No. It is enough to describe the situation and the decision you need help with. We will formulate the research questions together.

Yes. If you have reviews, tickets or interview recordings, I will first assess whether they are enough for your task. If not, I will suggest what to add.

I conduct interviews in Russian, Azerbaijani and English.
