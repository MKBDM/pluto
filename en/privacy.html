Privacy policy

How Pluto Digital Lab handles the data you leave on the site. Last updated: 11 November 2026.

1. Who processes the data

Pluto Digital Lab is an independent research practice. Contact: muradkadimov@pluto.az. The business is run by an individual entrepreneur.

2. What data is collected

The main pages of the site contain no forms and do not collect contact details themselves. The data you send via Telegram, WhatsApp, email or LinkedIn (name, contact for a reply, description of the task) reaches me through those services. A separate page, “Application to join” (for craftspeople I invite personally), contains a form: in it you provide your name, city, contact, workshop name, a link to Instagram or a website, details of your experience and current sales, one main product (category, name, prices, materials, technique, size and weight, production time), your capacity and readiness to ship orders abroad and, optionally, a comment. The form responses are recorded in a Google Sheets table, and I receive an email notification.

3. Cookies and analytics

With your consent, the site loads Google Analytics 4 (Google). It uses cookies and collects visit statistics: which pages are opened, device and browser type, approximate country, and the source of the visit. If you click “Decline”, analytics is not loaded and its cookies are not set. The choice itself is stored only in your browser. Change choice

4. Purpose

Only to reply to your request and arrange a call. Data from the “Application to join” form is used only to assess your products and to contact you. Data is not used for mailings without your consent.

5. Sharing with third parties

Data is not sold or passed to third parties, except to the services without which the site and email do not work. Responses to the “Application to join” form are stored in Google services (Google Sheets and email), which I use to receive them.

6. Retention period

Data you send via Telegram, WhatsApp, email or LinkedIn is kept for 6 months. After that it is deleted. Responses to the “Application to join” form are kept for 6 months if cooperation has not started; if it has started, retention terms are set by the contract. Google Analytics data (if you consented to cookies) is kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically.

7. Your rights

You can ask what data about you is stored, correct it or ask for it to be deleted — write to muradkadimov@pluto.az.

8. Client data in projects

Materials a client provides for research (reviews, interview recordings, tickets) are processed under a separate non-disclosure agreement (NDA) and contract, not under this policy.

9. Changes

If the policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
