Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data I receive through the Pluto Digital Lab website and communication channels, how I use it and how I store it. Last updated: 11 November 2026.

1. Who Processes Your Personal Data?

Pluto Digital Lab is my independent research practice. I operate as a sole proprietor and am responsible for the processing of personal data described in this policy. Contact: muradkadimov@pluto.az.

2. What Personal Data Do I Collect?

The main pages of my website do not contain contact forms. I receive information you send through Telegram, WhatsApp, email or LinkedIn via those services. This may include your name, contact details and a description of your enquiry.

A separate page titled “Etsy Collaboration Application”, intended for craftspeople I personally invite, contains a form. Through this form, I collect your name, city, contact details, workshop name, Instagram or website link, information about your experience and current sales, and details of one main product, including its category, name, prices, materials, production technique, dimensions, weight and production time. You may also provide information about your production capacity, readiness to ship orders internationally and an optional comment.

Form responses are stored in Google Sheets, and I receive an email notification when a new submission is received.

3. Cookies and Web Analytics

With your consent, I use Google Analytics 4 to analyse website traffic. This service uses cookies to collect visit statistics, including pages visited, device and browser type, approximate country and traffic source.

If you select “Decline”, Google Analytics will not load and its analytics cookies will not be set. Your consent preference is stored only in your browser.

Change Preference

4. How I Use Your Data

I use personal data received through communication channels solely to respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation. I use information submitted through the “Etsy Collaboration Application” form only to assess your products and contact you. I do not use your personal data for email campaigns or other promotional communications without your consent.

5. Sharing Data with Third Parties

I do not sell personal data or share it with third parties, except through services necessary to operate the website, email and enquiry submission process. I store responses to the “Etsy Collaboration Application” form using Google services, including Google Sheets and email.

6. Data Retention Periods

I retain data received through Telegram, WhatsApp, email or LinkedIn for 6 months, after which I delete it.

I retain responses to the “Etsy Collaboration Application” form for 6 months if no collaboration has started. If a collaboration begins, the applicable contract determines the data retention terms.

Google Analytics data collected with your consent is retained for 2 months and then automatically deleted.

7. Your Personal Data Rights

You can request information about the personal data I hold about you, ask for corrections or request its deletion. To exercise these rights, contact me at muradkadimov@pluto.az.

8. Client Data in Research Projects

I process client materials provided for research purposes, including customer reviews, interview recordings and support tickets, in accordance with a separate non-disclosure agreement (NDA) and service contract. The handling of such data is governed by those agreements rather than this policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

If I make changes to this policy, I will publish the updated version on this page with a revised update date.
