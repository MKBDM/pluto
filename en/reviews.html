  • Marketplace sellers and online shops — the rating is dropping and the reason is unclear
  • Mobile app teams — there are hundreds of store reviews and nobody to read them in full
  • Support and CX teams — there are many tickets, and management needs numbers and priorities
  • Owners of small and medium businesses — you need to decide what to improve without a long study
What you can send
Marketplace reviewsApp Store and Google Play reviewsSupport tickets and chatsAnswers to open survey and NPS questions

Format: Excel, CSV or Google Sheets. Review language: Russian, Azerbaijani or English. The volume of long support conversations is agreed after the free assessment.

What the report contains

Problems and a plan for next steps

  • A theme map: what customers write about and what repeats
  • Key problems with customer quotes; what needs checking is marked separately
  • Priorities: what to fix first and what it is linked to
  • Recommendations for solving the problems
  • A plan for further research and a proposal to continue working together
How the express analysis works
  1. Task. I receive your request and together we clarify what is missing for the brief
  2. Free assessment. I check whether the materials are suitable; if so, we confirm scope, timeline and cost
  3. Analysis. I group reviews by theme, find clusters of complaints, identify and prioritise problems
  4. Presentation and call. key problems and recommendations — in a format you can show your team right away
