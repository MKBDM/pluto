Who This Service Is For
  • Marketplace sellers and online retailers looking to understand the reasons behind low ratings and customer dissatisfaction.
  • Mobile app teams looking to organise user feedback and identify product improvement priorities.
  • CX and customer support teams looking to identify recurring issues through customer interactions.
  • Small and medium-sized businesses looking to improve customer experience without conducting a large-scale research study.
Data Sources I Analyse
Marketplace reviewsApp Store and Google Play reviewsSupport tickets and chat conversationsOpen-ended survey and NPS responses

I accept data in Excel, CSV and Google Sheets formats. Feedback can be in Azerbaijani, Russian or English. The scope of analysis for lengthy support conversations is agreed upon following the free initial assessment.

What the Report Includes

Problems and a plan for next steps

  • Thematic breakdown of customer feedback: key topics and recurring issues
  • Key customer issues supported by direct quotes, with hypotheses requiring further validation clearly identified
  • Improvement priorities: which issues to address first and the rationale behind their prioritisation
  • Actionable recommendations for addressing identified issues
  • Recommendations for further research and potential opportunities for continued collaboration
How Rapid Analysis Works
  1. Task: I clarify your business objectives, analysis goals and the key questions the research should address.
  2. Free initial assessment: I assess the suitability of your data and agree on the scope, timeline and cost of the analysis.
  3. Analysis: I categorise customer feedback, identify recurring issues and establish improvement priorities.
  4. Presentation and consultation: I deliver a presentation with key findings and actionable recommendations, followed by a 40-minute results consultation.
